The **Statistics** tab shows one day at a time. Use **Previous** and **Next** to move between days.

{{< screenshot src="/powerful_usage_statistics_1.png" alt="The Statistics tab, with Previous and Next buttons around today's date and a Flagged Content pie chart split by category." width="1920" height="1200" >}}

## Flagged Content

A pie chart of the images Aegis Explorer blocked that day, split by category. It gives you a sense of what your child is running into, without showing the pages or images themselves.

## Number of Events

Blocked images over the day, in five-minute intervals. Spikes show when filtering was most active.

{{< screenshot src="/powerful_usage_statistics_2.png" alt="The Number of Events and Active Times graphs on the Statistics tab, showing two browsing sessions, in the morning and the afternoon." width="1920" height="1200" >}}

## Active Times

A minute-by-minute record of when the extension was running. Chrome has to be open and in use for it to be recorded, so gaps are normal overnight or when the computer is idle. A long gap while your child was browsing can mean the extension was turned off or removed.

## What's recorded

The statistics only record **when** something was blocked and **which category** it was in. They don't record which websites were visited, or what the blocked image or text was.

Blocking records are kept for 30 days. All of this is stored only on the computer, in the extension's own storage, and is deleted if the extension is removed.
