Aegis Explorer is developed by **Aegis Technologies LLC**.

## Aegis Explorer

Aegis Explorer is a Chrome extension that detects and blocks potentially harmful images and text on web pages, using categories and sensitivity settings chosen by a parent.

It doesn't block entire websites. It checks the content of each page as it loads and hides only the images and text that fall into the selected categories. See [how it works](/#how-it-works) for details.

## Design philosophy

Aegis Explorer is purposely designed to strike a balance between online safety and independence:

- No filter is perfect; conversation and trust matter
- Not meant to be unbreakable, but **practical**
- Helps parents guide safe internet habits without extreme restrictions

The goal is mitigation, not total lockdown.

## Privacy

Settings and logs are stored on your computer. Only the content needed for classification is sent to our server, and it isn't saved. See the [privacy policy](/privacy/) for details.
